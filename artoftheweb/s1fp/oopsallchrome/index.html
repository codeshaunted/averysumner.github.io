If you don't care about security but want to use modern websites, you should use Google Chrome, or Edge, or Opera, or pretty much any mainstream browser besides Firefox or Safari. Most of the browsers on the market are actually just variations of Chrome under the hood. While some may make some security or privacy promises that Chrome doesn't, generally you, or more specifically your data, is the actual product for these browser companies. Google also recently effectively killed ad-blockers on Chrome, under the guise of a security update to their extension system.
