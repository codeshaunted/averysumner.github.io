## Data Collection
DALL-E Mini Discord Bot collects user IDs, server IDs and their associated prompt queries. These are stored indefinitely to prevent abuse of the model.

## Data Deletion
To request to have your data deleted, please send a request along with your user or server ID to [admin@codeshaunted.com](mailto:admin@codeshaunted.com).

## Privacy Modification
DALL-E Mini Discord Bot may revise this Privacy Policy for its bot at any time without prior notice.

## Questions
If you have any questions, you may contact us via email at [admin@codeshaunted.com](mailto:admin@codeshaunted.com).
